Your first week, answered.

Compass is the onboarding copilot that knows your department's playbook, and can read your screen.

The problem

Week one is a maze of systems, codes and "who do I ask?"

IT

VPN error 809, MFA, a laptop that will not connect. Screenshots in a chat, hours lost.

HR

Benefits windows, leave, offices. The answer exists, in a document nobody finds.

Consultants

Staffing, confidentiality, travel. The unwritten rules of a new firm.

Finance

Which booking code? Get it wrong and the timesheet bounces on Friday.

The demo, in five moves

Pick a department. Then just ask.

  1. Pick your department. You get a Day 1 / Week 1 / Month 1 checklist and a readiness score.
  2. Ask for a booking code. A lookup tool answers with the exact code, or says plainly there isn't one. It never invents one.
  3. Drop in a screenshot. Compass names the screen, gives the steps from the doc, cites it.
  4. Ask something the docs don't cover. An honest "I don't know", and a real ticket in the right queue.
  5. Ask it something ridiculous.
Because every good copilot has a sense of humour

"What is the purpose of life?"

"Per the Northwind strategic framework: to create shareholder value, ideally by end of quarter. Speaking of which, have you submitted your first timesheet yet?" Real output, verified live. Off-topic questions get the deadpan treatment, then a nudge back to onboarding: never a ticket, never a stiff refusal. Undocumented onboarding questions are a different case and still get an honest "I don't know".
