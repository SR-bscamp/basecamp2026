# Project staffing

Owner: Resource Management (staffing@northwind-advisory.example, ext 4400).

## How staffing works
- Resource Management matches consultants to engagements weekly. The staffing meeting is **Thursday 11:00am ET**; assignments are announced by email that afternoon.
- New hires are usually assigned within **2 weeks** of their start date (see `consultants/first-week.md`).
- Until you are assigned, book time to **INT-BEN-001** (bench). Bench time is non-billable.
- Your engagement manager confirms your client booking code in your welcome-to-the-project email. Codes are in `finance/booking-codes.md`.
