# Client confidentiality

Owner: Quality & Risk (INT-QA-001 for audit support time). Module: "Client Confidentiality", about 40 minutes, book to INT-TRN-001.

## Rules
- Every engagement has a signed NDA. Client names are confidential until the client approves publication.
- Do not name clients in public posts, job applications or conference talks.
- Keep client files only in the engagement folder on the approved file share; never on a personal drive or phone.
- Use code names in internal chat when discussing a prospect with no project code.
- Do not discuss one client's work with another client's team.

## If something goes wrong
Report a wrongly sent file or lost device to IT within 1 hour (`it/security-basics.md`) and tell your engagement manager the same day.

## Conflicts of interest
Declare any personal financial interest in a client to Quality & Risk before starting work. Declarations are reviewed within 5 business days.

## FAQ
**Can I mention a client on LinkedIn?** No, unless the client has approved publication.

**How long is the module?** About 40 minutes.

## Quick facts
- Report incidents within 1 hour. Conflict declarations reviewed within 5 business days.
