# Consultants: your first week

Owner: Resource Management (staffing@northwind-advisory.example, ext 4400). Checklist item id: `first_week`. Applies to analysts and managers.

## Day 1 (Monday)
- 9:00am ET: welcome session with People & Learning (book to INT-ADM-001).
- Collect your laptop from IT, then **enroll in MFA within 24 hours** (`it/mfa-setup.md`) and set up the VPN (`it/vpn-setup.md`).
- Meet your buddy: every new consultant is paired with a buddy from the same practice for 90 days.

## Week 1
- Wednesday: Northwind Methods workshop, 2 hours. Book to **INT-TRN-001** (internal training, non-billable).
- Read `finance/booking-codes.md`. Your engagement manager tells you which client code applies to you; if you are not assigned yet, book waiting time to **INT-BEN-001** (bench).
- Submit your first timesheet by **Friday 5:00pm ET** (`finance/timesheets.md`).
- Friday 3:00pm ET: 30-minute check-in with your engagement manager.

## Month 1
- Enroll in benefits within 30 days of your start date (`hr/benefits-enrollment.md`).
- Complete the 4 mandatory e-learning modules: Security Basics, Client Confidentiality, Code of Conduct, Time Recording. Each takes about 40 minutes; book to INT-TRN-001.
- 30-day review with your engagement manager.

## Dress and hours
Core hours are 10:00am-4:00pm local time. Dress code is business casual unless the client says otherwise. Travel and expense policy is not covered in this guide.

## FAQ
**Which code is the Methods workshop?** INT-TRN-001.

**I have no project yet. What do I book?** Waiting time goes to INT-BEN-001; onboarding sessions to INT-ADM-001. Resource Management assigns projects, usually within the first two weeks.

**Who is my buddy?** Your welcome email names your buddy. If it does not, ask Resource Management on ext 4400.

**When do I get paid?** This guide does not cover payroll dates. Ask the HR People Team or open an HR ticket.

**Can I work from home?** Core hours apply wherever you work. Client engagements may require on-site days; your engagement manager confirms them.

**What is the order of Day 1 tasks?** Laptop, then MFA (within 24 hours), then VPN, then read this guide.
