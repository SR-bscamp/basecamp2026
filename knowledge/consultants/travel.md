# Client travel

Owner: Resource Management (ext 4400) with Finance Operations (ext 4300).

## Booking
- Book flights and hotels through the Northwind Travel portal (https://travel.northwind-advisory.example, MFA required) at least **7 days** before departure.
- Get your engagement manager's approval before booking; approval is recorded in the portal.
- Flights under 6 hours: economy class. Rail is preferred for trips under 3 hours.
- Book rental cars only when public transport is not practical, and only with manager approval.

## During the trip
- Record travel time to the client's booking code only when it is agreed in the engagement terms; otherwise record it to INT-ADM-001 and check with your engagement manager.
- Keep receipts and claim within 30 days (`finance/expenses.md`).
- Connect to the VPN on hotel and client networks (`it/vpn-setup.md`). If you see error 809, switch to NW-Gateway-Alt.

## Emergencies
Call the 24-hour travel line on ext 4450. For a lost laptop call ext 4000 immediately.

## Not covered here
Meal allowances are not covered in this document.

## FAQ
**How far ahead do I book?** At least 7 days.

**What class of flight?** Economy for flights under 6 hours.

## Quick facts
- Book 7 days ahead via the Travel portal. Travel emergency line ext 4450.
