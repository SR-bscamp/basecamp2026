# Leave and holidays

Owner: HR People Team (people@northwind-advisory.example, ext 4100, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm ET).

## Annual leave
- Full-time employees get **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Leave accrues monthly; in your first year it is pro-rated from your start date.
- You can carry over up to **5 days** into the next year; they expire on 31 March.
- Request leave in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for 3 or more days. Your line manager approves within 2 business days.
- Book approved leave and public holidays on your timesheet to **INT-PTO-001** (8 hours per day).

## Sick leave
- **10 paid sick days** per year. Tell your manager before 10:00am local time on the first day.
- A doctor's note is needed from the 3rd consecutive day.
- Book sick days to INT-PTO-001.

## Other leave
- Bereavement leave: 3 days for immediate family.
- Jury duty: paid, with the summons shown to HR.
- Parental and family leave entitlements are not described in this document. Ask the HR People Team or open an HR ticket.

## Public holidays
Northwind observes the public holidays of the country where you work. Client engagements may require work on a holiday only with your agreement.

## FAQ
**How many annual leave days do I get?** 20 per calendar year.

**Do unused days pay out?** Up to 5 days carry over; the rest are lost on 31 March unless HR agrees otherwise in writing.

**Which code do I book leave to?** INT-PTO-001.

## Quick facts
- Annual leave 20 days; carry-over 5 days until 31 March; sick leave 10 days; bereavement 3 days.
- Notice: 2 weeks for 3 or more days; approval within 2 business days.
