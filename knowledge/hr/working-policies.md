# Working policies

Owner: HR People Team (ext 4100).

## Hours and flexibility
- Core hours 10:00am-4:00pm local time. Standard week 40 hours.
- Hybrid: 2 office days a week for staff not on a client site. Anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday.
- Overtime is not paid for salaried consultants; time over 40 hours is still recorded on the timesheet.

## Probation and reviews
- Probation lasts **90 days**. Review points: 30-day, 60-day and 90-day with your line manager.
- Performance reviews run twice a year, in June and December.

## Conduct
- The Code of Conduct (e-learning module, about 40 minutes) is mandatory in month 1.
- Raise concerns with the HR People Team or the confidential Speak Up line: speakup@northwind-advisory.example. Retaliation is a disciplinary offence.

## Notice and exits
Notice period is 1 month for analysts and 2 months for managers. Return your laptop to IT on your last day.

## FAQ
**How long is probation?** 90 days.

**Which days am I in the office?** Tuesday and Thursday, unless you are on a client site.

**When are reviews?** June and December.

## Quick facts
- Probation 90 days. Notice 1 month (analyst), 2 months (manager). Office days Tue and Thu.
