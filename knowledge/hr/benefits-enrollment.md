# Benefits enrollment

Owner: HR Benefits Team (benefits@northwind-advisory.example, ext 4200, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm ET). Checklist item id: `benefits`.

## Deadlines
- New hires have **30 calendar days from their start date** to enroll. Miss it and you wait for open enrollment (1-15 November each year), with coverage starting 1 January.
- Coverage starts on the **first day of the month after you enroll**. Example: enrolling on 12 March starts coverage on 1 April.
- Qualifying life events (marriage, birth or adoption, loss of other coverage) give a **60-day** window to change plans.

## How to enroll
1. Connect to the VPN (`it/vpn-setup.md`) and sign in to https://benefits.northwind-advisory.example with MFA.
2. Open **My Benefits**, then **Start enrollment**.
3. Pick a medical plan, optionally dental and vision, and add dependants. Dependants need a date of birth and, for spouses, a marriage date.
4. Choose your retirement contribution percentage.
5. Review and click **Submit**. You get a confirmation number starting with BEN-. Keep it.

## Retirement plan
The Northwind Retirement Plan matches **100% of the first 4%** of salary you contribute. You are eligible from day one. Default contribution if you do not choose is 3%.

## Not covered here
This document covers enrollment only. It does not describe leave entitlements, parental leave, expense limits or payroll dates. For those, ask the HR People Team (people@northwind-advisory.example, ext 4100) or open an HR ticket.

## FAQ
**I missed the 30-day window. What now?** You can enroll only during open enrollment (1-15 November) unless you have a qualifying life event. Contact the HR Benefits Team to confirm your situation.

**Can I change my plan later?** Yes, during open enrollment or within 60 days of a qualifying life event.

**When does my coverage start if I enroll on my start date?** On the first day of the following month. A person starting on 5 January who enrolls the same day is covered from 1 February.

**Where do I find my confirmation?** The portal shows it under **My Benefits > History**. Confirmation numbers start with BEN-.

**Who can I add as a dependant?** Spouse or domestic partner and children under 26. You need each person's date of birth.

**Do I need the VPN?** Yes. The benefits portal is only reachable on the VPN or the office network, and requires MFA.

**Is dental included in medical?** No. Dental Basic and Vision are separate elections.
