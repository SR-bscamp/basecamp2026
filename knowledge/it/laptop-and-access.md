# Laptop and system access

Owner: IT Helpdesk (helpdesk@northwind-advisory.example, ext 4000, Mon-Fri 7am-7pm ET).

## Your laptop
- Collect it from IT on Day 1 (Monday, 8:30am-9:00am ET, before the welcome session). Bring photo ID.
- Standard model: Northwind Standard Laptop (Windows 11 or macOS; analysts get Windows unless they ask for macOS). Full-disk encryption is on and cannot be turned off.
- Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Report damage to the IT queue within 1 business day.
- Lost or stolen: call ext 4000 immediately (see `it/vpn-setup.md`).

## Requesting extra access
Open an IT ticket (queue IT) with the system name, your manager's name and the business reason. Your manager must approve by replying to the ticket. Standard requests are done within 2 business days; access to client file shares needs the engagement manager's approval and takes up to 3 business days.

## FAQ
**How do I reset my password?** At https://account.northwind-advisory.example/reset. Passwords expire every 180 days.

**Can I use my own laptop?** No. Client data may only be opened on Northwind laptops.

**Can I install software?** Only items in the Northwind Software Catalog (Company Portal app). Anything else needs an IT ticket.
