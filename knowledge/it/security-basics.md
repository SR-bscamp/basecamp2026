# Security basics

Owner: IT Helpdesk (ext 4000) with Quality & Risk. The e-learning module "Security Basics" (about 40 minutes, book to INT-TRN-001) covers this.

## Rules everyone follows
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop (Windows+L or Ctrl+Cmd+Q). The screen locks automatically after 5 minutes.
- Never share passwords or MFA codes, including with IT.
- Use only Northwind email for client work. Do not forward client files to personal accounts.
- USB storage is blocked by default.
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only while connected to the VPN (`it/vpn-setup.md`).

## Reporting a phishing email
Click **Report phishing** in the mail client. Do not click links or open attachments. If you already clicked, call ext 4000 at once and open an IT ticket marked urgent. IT replies to phishing reports within 1 hour during support hours.

## Incidents
A lost laptop, a wrongly sent client file or a suspected account compromise must be reported to IT **within 1 hour** of noticing it. Reporting quickly is never penalised.

## FAQ
**How often is Security Basics repeated?** Every 12 months.

**Can I take a work photo of a client whiteboard?** Only with the client's permission, and store it in the engagement folder, not on your phone.

## Quick facts
- Auto-lock 5 minutes. Report incidents within 1 hour. Training yearly, 40 minutes.
