# Expenses

Owner: Finance Operations (timesheets@northwind-advisory.example, ext 4300).

## Submitting claims
- Submit in the expense app (https://expenses.northwind-advisory.example, MFA required) within **30 days** of the spend.
- Attach a receipt for every item of **$25 or more**. Under $25 no receipt is needed but you still list the item.
- Claims are approved by your line manager within 3 business days and paid in the next monthly payroll run.
- Billable client expenses must carry the client booking code (see `finance/booking-codes.md`), for example CLI-BLU-2402. Non-billable expenses use the relevant INT- code.

## What is not reimbursed
Alcohol, personal entertainment, fines and traffic penalties, and upgrades chosen for comfort.

## Company card
Employees at manager level and above receive a company card. Analysts pay personally and claim back.

## Not covered here
Meal allowances and per-diem rates are not covered in this document. Travel booking is in `consultants/travel.md`. Ask Finance Operations or open an HR ticket.

## FAQ
**How long do I have to claim?** 30 days.

**Do I need a receipt for a $12 taxi?** No; list it without a receipt.

**Who approves my claim?** Your line manager.

## Quick facts
- Claim within 30 days. Receipt from $25. Approval 3 business days. Contact ext 4300.
