# Timesheets

Owner: Finance Operations (ext 4300). Checklist item id: `timesheet`. Booking codes are in `finance/booking-codes.md`.

## Weekly deadline
- Submit your timesheet **every Friday by 5:00pm ET** for the Monday-Friday week.
- Your manager approves by **Monday 12:00 noon ET**. Unapproved timesheets delay client invoicing.
- A standard week is 40 hours. Enter time in 0.25-hour (15-minute) steps. Maximum 14 hours on one day.

## Submit step by step
1. Connect to the VPN (`it/vpn-setup.md`) and open https://time.northwind-advisory.example with MFA.
2. Choose the current week.
3. Click **Add line**, type or pick the booking code, and enter hours for each day.
4. Repeat for each code. Training, admin and bench time are separate lines.
5. Click **Validate**. Fix any red lines, then click **Submit**. The status becomes "Submitted - awaiting approval".

## Error: "Invalid booking code"
A red banner reading "Invalid booking code: line not saved" means the code on that line is not in the code list. Common causes: a typo, a missing hyphen, lowercase letters, a code for a project you are not staffed on, or a made-up code. Fix it:
1. Look up the exact code in `finance/booking-codes.md`.
2. Retype it with hyphens and capital letters, for example `INT-TRN-001`.
3. If the project is not in the list, do not guess. Ask your engagement manager and book the hours to INT-BEN-001 or INT-ADM-001 meanwhile, as agreed with them.

## Late and missed timesheets
- Submitting after Friday 5:00pm ET but before Monday noon ET is "late"; it is accepted but flagged.
- After Monday noon ET use the **Correction request** form on the timesheet site; Finance Operations processes it within 2 business days.
- First-week timesheet: even if you only attended onboarding, submit it. Book onboarding to INT-ADM-001.

## Leave and holidays
Book public holidays and paid leave to INT-PTO-001 (8 hours per day).

## FAQ
**When is the deadline?** Friday 5:00pm ET each week. Approval is due Monday noon ET.

**How many hours can I enter per day?** Up to 14, in 0.25-hour steps.

**Can I edit after submitting?** Until your manager approves, yes: click **Recall**. After approval use the Correction request form.

**Who approves my timesheet?** Your engagement manager (consultants) or line manager (everyone else).

**What if I worked on something without a code?** Ask your engagement manager. Do not invent a code, because an invalid code blocks the line from saving.

**Where do I see my status?** The timesheet site shows Draft, Submitted - awaiting approval, Approved, or Returned. A Returned timesheet has a comment from your manager; fix and resubmit within one business day.

**Do I need the VPN?** Yes, away from the office.
