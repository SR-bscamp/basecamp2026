# Compass UI: "Field Atlas"

Onboarding is a journey through unfamiliar territory, so Compass looks like a printed expedition atlas, not a dark SaaS dashboard.

- **Paper and ink.** Warm paper (#F3EFE6), near-black ink, hairline rules instead of cards. No glows, no rounded-card grid, almost no pills.
- **Type.** Instrument Serif for display (huge numerals, headings, the hero), Instrument Sans for reading, JetBrains Mono for data and file paths. Hierarchy comes from scale: one enormous serif number per screen.
- **Departments are territories.** Each has its own ink colour *and* terrain drawn in SVG: IT is a circuit grid (cobalt), HR is contour rings (terracotta), Consultants is a triangulation survey (teal), Finance is ledger ruling (ochre). Terrain fills the picker tiles and sits behind the hero.
- **The checklist is a route.** Day 1, Week 1 and Month 1 are waypoints on a dashed trail; the hero is "62% of the way". A checked stop is stamped; reaching 100% stamps "Ready."
- **Answers are field notes.** The reply, numbered steps (serif numerals), what Compass did (tool trace) and footnoted sources read as one object. Sources open the full document in a side sheet.
- **Icons** are Lucide line icons only. Motion is ink-like: lines draw, stamps land, text types in as it streams.
